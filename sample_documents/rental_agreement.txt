COMMERCIAL LEASE AND RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Commercial Rental Agreement (the "Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of October, 2026 (the "Commencement Date"), by and between:

PARTIES:
1. Mr. Vikram Oberoi, son of Late Sh. S. K. Oberoi, residing at Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Landlord" or "Lessor").
2. NextGen Analytics LLP, a registered limited liability partnership having its registered office at Madhapur, Hyderabad, represented by its Designated Partner Ms. Priya Nair (hereinafter called the "Tenant" or "Lessee").

1. PURPOSE & PREMISES:
The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the commercial office space located at Suite 402, Cyber Tower, Hitec City, Hyderabad, admeasuring 2,500 square feet, solely for the purpose of running an information technology and data consulting office.

2. DURATION / LEASE PERIOD:
The lease is granted for a fixed duration of eleven (11) months, starting from 01-10-2026 and expiring on 31-08-2027. The lease may be renewed for a further period subject to mutual agreement and an 8% escalation in monthly rent.

3. PAYMENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT:
- Monthly Rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 65,000 (Rupees Sixty-Five Thousand Only), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every calendar month.
- Security Deposit: The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 3,00,000 (Rupees Three Lakhs Only) with the Landlord, refundable upon peaceful handover of premises.
- Utilities: Electricity, water, and broadband charges shall be borne directly by the Tenant based on consumption.

4. KEY OBLIGATIONS:
- Tenant Obligations: Maintain the leased premises in good, tenantable condition; not make major structural alterations without prior written consent; use premises strictly for IT office purposes.
- Landlord Obligations: Ensure peaceful and unhindered possession of premises; maintain exterior building structure and common area facilities.

5. TERMINATION & NOTICE PERIOD:
- Either party may terminate this Agreement prior to expiry by giving two (2) months (60 days) advance written notice to the other party.
- If Tenant defaults on rental payments for two consecutive months, Landlord may terminate this lease with seven (7) days notice and repossess the property.

6. POTENTIALLY IMPORTANT CLAUSES:
- Maintenance & Repairs: Day-to-day minor maintenance is Tenant responsibility; major structural damage is Landlord responsibility.
- Sub-letting: The Tenant shall not sub-let, assign, or transfer possession of the premises to any third party.
- Dispute Resolution: Governing law is the laws of Telangana, India. Jurisdiction of courts at Hyderabad.
